Buying a ticket takes a few minutes and needs no registration: you choose the seats, pay by card, and the tickets arrive by e-mail.

  1. Choose the event

    Open the events listing and pick an event. The event page shows the date, venue, age limit, running time and prices.

  2. Choose your tickets

    Below the poster, choose the tickets. If the event has a seating plan, click the seats you want; otherwise choose the ticket category and the number of tickets. The price is shown at once.

  3. Leave your e-mail

    Enter the e-mail address the tickets should go to. Name and phone are optional. Check the address carefully — the tickets are sent there.

  4. Pay by card

    Accept the terms and press «Pay». You are taken to the secure payment page: enter your card details and confirm the payment. Card data is handled by Stripe; we never see it. The chosen seats are held for you for 30 minutes — if you do not pay in that time, they go back on sale.

  5. Get your tickets

    Right after payment the tickets arrive by e-mail as a PDF with a QR code. Show it at the entrance on your phone or printed — either works. If the letter has not arrived within a few minutes, check the spam folder.

My account

An account is created for you automatically with the e-mail you entered. In My account you can see your orders, download the tickets again or re-send them to your e-mail.
