How to connect your Stripe account to ArenaSoldOut platform

To integrate Stripe as the payment gateway for Arenasoldout.com, you need to invite a technician spetialist of the platform, to join your Stripe account as a developer. Follow these steps:

Step-by-Step Instructions

Log in to Stripe Settings

  • Access your Stripe account and navigate to the dashboard.
  • Click on the gear icon or the “Settings” option in the lower-left corner of the dashboard.

Select the ‘Team’ Menu

  • In the settings menu, find and select the “Team” option.
  • This will take you to the team management page where you can manage users and their roles.

Add a Team Member as Developer

  • On the team management page, click the “Add member” button.

  • Enter the following details:

    • Email: ava@abhteam.com
    • Role: Developer

  • The Developer role allows the invited person to manage API keys and integration settings, which is essential for setting up Stripe as the payment gateway.

Send the Invitation

  • After filling in the required details, send the invitation.
  • Andrei Andreev will receive an email with instructions to join your Stripe account.

Developer Role Explanation

The Developer role in Stripe is crucial for managing technical aspects of the integration, including API keys and webhooks. By inviting Andrei Andreev as a developer, you ensure that he has the necessary permissions to set up and configure Stripe for seamless payment processing on your Stripe account. This role does not have access to sensitive financial information but can handle all the technical requirements needed for integration.
