## Style notes

- Address form follows the existing WP texts: formal in cs, de, lt, hu, uk, ru; informal (second person singular) in pl, et, fi, lv.
- Placeholders (`%d`, `%s`, `%1$s`, `{name}`) stay byte-identical in every language.
- The plural key `tour.cities` is an array of three forms [one, few, many], as in the WP source.
- The ten non-English terms for "Tickets" are the buyer-facing words already on the old site; do not replace them with calques.
